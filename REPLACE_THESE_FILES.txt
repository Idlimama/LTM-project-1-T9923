REPLACE THESE FILES IN THE marketing-governance FOLDER

1. backend\providers\ollama_provider.py
2. backend\orchestration\graph.py
3. backend\services\evaluator.py
4. index.html

Do not delete any other project files.

After replacement, run from the project root:

.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m py_compile backend\providers\ollama_provider.py backend\orchestration\graph.py backend\services\evaluator.py
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest -q
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m uvicorn backend.main:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000

Then press Ctrl+F5 in the browser.

This hotfix limits the live path to at most two Ollama calls:
- structured campaign extraction
- one combined four-specialist council call

The second council synthesis is deterministic so that hard gates remain stable and the live review does not spend several additional minutes on reflection calls.
